How to use the chat services

When prompting the virtual assistant

Type your question in the message box and select the icon or Enter to send.

To help the GenAI chatbot find the information you need:

  • Ask your questions in complete sentences
  • Avoid using slang or jargon
  • Describe your situation without sharing personal information
  • Focus on one topic per question

If you were unable to get the answer you were looking for:

  • Be specific and clear when asking questions, for example, ask “When are Individual taxes due?” not, “When are taxes due?”
  • If you have multiple questions, ask them one at a time

Available topics for live agent chat

Live agents in My Account can answer both account-specific and general questions about:

  • Personal Income Tax
  • Benefits
  • My Account
  • Ordering CRA forms and publications
  • Payments to the CRA
  • Savings and Pension Plans
  • Instalments

Unavailable topics for live agent chat

Live agents in My Account are unable to assist with questions about:

  • Rental Income
  • Estates and Trusts
  • Deceased individuals
  • Non-resident returns or enquiries
  • Foreign income reporting
  • Capital Gains
  • Crypto-assets
  • My Business Account
  • Represent a Client
  • Business income or expenses

Questions regarding these topics must continue to be directed towards our phone lines.

Protect your privacy

Virtual assistant

Personal information will not be collected or stored when interacting with the virtual assistant inside My Account.

Privacy notice

Do not share any personal details like your name, address, social insurance number (SIN), date of birth, or financial information while using the chatbot. The GenAI chatbot is not designed to securely handle personal or sensitive information. During your interaction with the GenAI chatbot, your questions may be temporarily retained to enhance your chatbot experience. Your personal information, chat history, or other interactions are not stored or used. For more details on privacy, please refer to our Privacy Statement.

Handling of personal information

Users are advised not to enter personal information when interacting with the GenAI chatbot. If you enter personal information during the interaction, the CRA will check to ensure it aligns with section 4 of the Privacy Act. Any unauthorized information will be promptly deleted to reduce privacy risks. The GenAI chatbot can usually identify questions containing personal information, which may help in deleting such data.

Important information for users

The information provided by the GenAI chatbot is based on Canada.ca webpages and should not be taken as professional, legal, or medical advice. Information from the GenAI chatbot may not be fully accurate or meet your specific needs. Always use your best judgment and consult human professionals or other online sources before making decisions based on this information. Use this information at your own risk.

Live agent

Privacy notice

Personal information (including the SIN) is collected and used to administer or enforce the Income Tax Act, Excise Tax Act, other federal statutes, and related programs and activities including administering tax, benefits, audit, compliance, and collection. The information collected may be disclosed to other federal, provincial, territorial, aboriginal, or foreign government institutions to the extent authorized by law. Under the Privacy Act, individuals have a right of protection, access to and correction of their personal information, or to file a complaint with the Privacy Commissioner of Canada regarding the handling of their personal information. Personal information is described in program-specific Personal Information Banks which can be found on the CRA’s Information about Programs and Information Holdings (Info Source) page.

Transcripts of online chats will be stored for quality assurance, verification, and security purposes.
