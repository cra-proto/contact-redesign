What do you want to do?

  • Access or change your CRA information or account
  • Understand your taxes, benefits and credits
  • Payments made or received

    What kind payment do you need help with?

    • The CRA is sending you money
    • You are sending, or sent the CRA money

      What do you need help with regarding sending money or sent money to the CRA?

      • The right payment method for the amount owed
      • Due date for the amount owed
      • Confirming or correcting a payment
      • Paying a debt over time
      • Transfering a payment to another account
  • Sending or receiving documentation
  • Business topics
  • None of the above

Here is how to confirm or correct a payment

Result


Online

You may be able to resolve your question without calling.

CRA account

Use your individual or business CRA Account to:

  • Review payments you sent or expect to receive
  • Correct a payment that went to the wrong place
  • Make a payment to the CRA
  • Chat online with a service agent
  • Request a payment search or a refund search (business account only)
  • CRA sign in
  • Register for a CRA account

By phone

Individual tax account
Information you will need before you call
  • Social Insurance Number
  • Full name and date of birth
  • Complete address
  • An assessed tax return, notice of assessment, other tax document, or be signed in to your CRA account
Calling on behalf of someone else

If you’re calling on behalf of someone else, they must be present on the call to give their consent, or you must be authorized to call on their behalf.

Learn more: Authorize a representative

Alternative phone numbers

Specific phone numbers for certain situations:

  • Yukon, Northwest Territories and Nunavut residents

    1-866-426-1527 (opens up phone application)

    Only use this number if your area code is 867. If your area code is something else, call the general number at 1-800-959-8281.

    Service hours for 1-866-426-1527 are the same as those mentioned above for live service agents. You will be connected almost immediately.

  • Located outside of Canada and the U.S.

    1-613-940-8495 (opens up phone application)

    The CRA only accepts collect calls made through a phone operator. Once your call is connected, you might hear a beep and experience a short delay.

    Wait times and service hours are the same as those mentioned above for live service agents.

  • TTY (Teletypewriter) and Canada VRS (Video Relay Service)
    TTY number
    1-800-665-0354
    Canada VRS registration

    Download the VRS Canada app to use the VRS phone line.

    For more information, refer to Canada VRS.

    You will be connected with a live agent, however there is no wait time for these services.

    Service hours are the same as those mentioned above for live service agents. You will be connected almost immediately.
